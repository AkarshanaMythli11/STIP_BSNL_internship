Project Abstract & Scope

The rapid growth of telecommunication services has increased the demand for efficient planning and optimal utilization of network infrastructure. Telecom service providers often face challenges in deciding which geographical areas should be prioritized for network expansion or infrastructure upgrades due to limited resources and increasing customer demands.

To address this issue, STIP automates data processing and visualization, providing telecom engineers with a data-driven approach to prioritize areas requiring network improvement. The system leverages the Telecom Infrastructure Priority Index (TIPI) score calculated via a weighted multi-criteria algorithm to identify circles in critical need of expansion, optimizing resource allocation.

Key Core Modules

Area Management

Maintain detailed geographical profiles of circles including population demographics, transceiver status, and fiber details.

Priority Calculator

Runs a normalized, weighted scoring model mapping multi-dimensional factors to a consolidated TIPI priority index score (0-100).

Interactive Analytics

Rich graphic charts tracking technology distribution, complaint heatmaps, and correlation plots between populations and index values.

Report Generation

Export planning indexes, circle statistics, and concrete infrastructure recommendations to Excel, PDF, or direct Print sheets.

Decision Support Parameters

The TIPI algorithm evaluates the following weights to determine priority scores:

Customer Complaints (20%)

Prioritizes regions with high ticket volumes and poor user experience.

Signal Strength (20%)

Inverse-weighted to prioritize poor RF coverage zones.

Circle Population (15%)

Maximizes ROI by weighting highly populated rural and urban zones.

Network Technology (15%)

Weights legacy networks (2G/3G) to target 4G/5G upgrade cycles.
